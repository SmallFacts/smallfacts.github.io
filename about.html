About me

Field experience and geographic information science

I’m Brodde Stanford, a Geographic Information Science & Technology student at Texas A&M University.

My background

My experience includes field equipment maintenance, troubleshooting, geological sample analysis, and drilling documentation. As a mud logger, my work includes monitoring drilling operations and recording lithology, gas readings, and drilling parameters.

My field service experience includes maintaining, repairing, programming, and troubleshooting technical equipment, operating heavy equipment, and providing on-call support.

In my field geography class and advanced GIS courses, I have used ArcGIS Field Maps and ArcGIS Pro. I also have a basic understanding of Java, Python, and HTML.

My project work

In The New World Screwworm project, my workstream is environmental spatial context and data comparability. My contribution addresses environmental conditions, alternative explanations, and information that could reduce uncertainty.

This work includes collecting and preparing environmental data, assessing whether sources support comparable summaries, and documenting missing data and limits on interpretation. The project uses AI-assisted scripts, checks, and draft interpretation.
