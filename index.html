Featured project

The New World Screwworm

Assessing environmental datasets to understand what comparisons they support—and where uncertainty remains.

My contribution examined environmental conditions, alternative explanations, and information gaps using data from NASA POWER, NOAA, and Guatemala’s INSIVUMEH. The work included environmental data collection and preparation, checks on data comparability, and interpretation of descriptive results.

For September 2026, the sampled Texas grid centres had a higher median temperature and lower median relative humidity than the sampled centres in Mexico and Guatemala. These results describe the selected grid estimates, not regional area averages or conditions at detection sites. They do not establish a transmission mechanism or risk ranking.

Contribution: Brodde Stanford. Source compilation, metadata joins, and contextual guidance: Brandon Santor. Scripts, checks, and draft interpretation were prepared with Codex assistance.
