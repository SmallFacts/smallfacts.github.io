New_NWSW · Environmental research

Environmental spatial context and data comparability

My contribution examines environmental conditions, alternative explanations, and information gaps using NASA POWER, NOAA, and INSIVUMEH data.

The work includes environmental data collection and preparation, assessment of missing records and reporting periods, and descriptive comparisons of selected grid centres in Guatemala, Mexico, and Texas.

Prepared with Codex assistance for scripts, checks, and draft interpretation. Findings describe selected environmental estimates and do not establish causation.
